# B2.5 Geodesic aerobic methanotrophy

## Reaction and accounting

Methanotrophs use CH4 and O2 in their current Geodesic ocean cell and layer. Catabolism follows **CH4 + 2 O2 → CO2 + 2 H2O**. The simplified assimilation model uses **CH4 + O2 → CH2O + H2O** as a carbon balance. CH2O represents a unit of stored organic carbon and approximates biomass composition. Water is implicit. Only the oxidized share earns energy.

The existing methane uptake, oxygen budget, energy and assimilation settings refer to a 0.5 simulated-second reference tick. With methane and oxygen budgets of `0.01` each, an energy parameter of `0.04` and an assimilation fraction of `0.15`, a full-performance tick can withdraw approximately **0.0054054 CH4** and **0.0100 O2**, add **0.0008108** to the organism store, return **0.0045946 CO2** and add up to **0.0183784 energy**. Less free store capacity reduces the assimilation share and its oxygen demand. The unused methane remains dissolved while the planned catabolic share can still react.

```text
CH4 withdrawn ≈ CO2 returned + increase in stored organic carbon
O2 withdrawn ≈ 2 × CO2 returned + increase in stored organic carbon
energy gained ≤ min(CO2 returned, (O2 withdrawn − increase in stored organic carbon) / 2)
                × energy parameter / configured CH4 uptake
```

The existing node-level competition allocates dissolved methane and oxygen across consumers. The resource field rechecks both budgets before writing, using the measured aerobic-carbon transaction from saprotrophy. Store and energy increments round down to representable floats. Resource writes are planned in double precision and applied together after the debit and product checks pass. The relative tolerance for each bounded resource transfer is `2e-6`. Invalid or unrepresentable transfers leave resources and organism credits unchanged. An already established methanotroph can feed below the founding and mutation thresholds as long as local CH4 and O2 remain positive and can be transferred. Its experienced temperature scales uptake. The same reaction runs in light and darkness. Legacy Cube Sphere continues to use its existing reaction scaffold and coefficients.

B2.4 methanogens credit the same dissolved CH4 inventory that this reaction debits. Photosynthesis credits the same local O2 inventory. Vertical and horizontal ocean transport and air–sea exchange can change where those gases coexist. OrganicC stays in the organism store until the existing lifecycle uses or recycles it.

## Evolution and startup

Hydrogenotrophy → Methanotrophy follows the Legacy parent relationship and independent probability, `0.0015` by default. Legacy uses local oxygen as the unlock, with no timed evolution unlock. Geodesic rolls after the earlier metabolism choices and before the independent saprotrophy roll, which can still take precedence. The daughter requires Methanotrophy availability, a viable reaction recipe, positive dissolved CH4 and O2 at or above the Geodesic concentration thresholds, and positive experienced-temperature performance in the destination template. The default gates are **CH4 0.001** and **O2 0.01**. The default template is **7–47 °C** (280.15–320.15 K). The daughter keeps ordinary inherited traits, takes the destination thermal template and lethal margin, and then undergoes the normal temperature mutation step. Methanotrophy has its own seeded mutation stream.

In **New Game → Biology**, enable **Methanotrophy** or use **All implemented pathways**. **Metabolic evolution** exposes the Hydrogen → Methanotrophy probability and both local concentration thresholds. The O2 threshold is shared with aerobic saprotrophy acquisition. Choose **Methanotrophy** in the reaction parameter selector for CH4 uptake, O2 budget, energy and assimilation. The thermal template is under **Initial / inherited temperature preferences**. Methanotrophs use the Legacy pink base colour and appear in the existing **Methanotroph** population HUD row.

**Experimental methanotroph founders** defaults to `0`, including for older startup files. A positive count requires Methanotrophy and Passive drift. Available capacity prompts a bounded search once per observed ocean transport tick. The search moves through a limited range of nodes each tick and checks local CH4, O2 and experienced temperature at layer centers. It uses the first eligible site found, with a separate seeded RNG for founder traits. If the cap permits only part of the batch, the remainder stays pending and can be placed when capacity opens and a site qualifies. The startup availability summary reports `0/requested`, and each successful placement reports the cumulative count, remaining count, cell and layer in `[GeodesicMethanotrophFounders]`. Founders start with ordinary founder energy and an empty carbon store.

## Manual Unity validation

The settings below are for two independent user-run scenarios. Use a temporary startup profile or restore the prior values afterward. Leave **Hydrogen → fermentation probability** at the saved **0.02**.

### Scenario A — Isolated aerobic methanotrophy

1. In **New Game**, choose **Geodesic Icosphere**, turn off **Use Random Seed**, and set **Planet Seed** to `12345`. In **Planet**, set **Axis Tilt** to `0°` and **Day Length** to `60` simulated seconds. In **Advanced**, set **Geodesic subdivision** to `3`, choose **Ocean World**, use a minimum depth of `0.02` planet units, and select a resource transport interval of `5` simulated seconds.
2. In **Ocean**, set initial dissolved **CH4 0.01**, **O2 0.10**, **CO2 0.10**, and **Fe2+ 0**. These methane and oxygen concentrations are ten times their default acquisition thresholds. Set **Base Temperature** to `293.15 K`, **Insolation Temperature Gain** to `0 K`, and the **Global vent production multiplier** to `0`. In **Atmosphere**, set O2, CH4, H2 and H2S fractions to `0` and the air–sea exchange half-life to `0` so the dissolved inputs stay isolated from atmospheric exchange.
3. In **Biology**, enable only **Methanotrophy** and **Passive drift**. Set **Initial replicator count** and the other founder counts to `0`, **Experimental methanotroph founders** to `5`, **Maximum population** to `20`, and **Reproduction rate** and **Basal maintenance energy / sim s** to `0`. Set the Methanotrophy template to `0–100 °C`, the minimum local CH4 and O2 to `0.001` and `0.01`, the CH4 budget, O2 budget, energy and assimilation to `0.01 / 0.01 / 0.04 / 0.15`, and **Maximum organic carbon store** to `1`.
4. Temporarily enable **Biology Diagnostics → Enabled** and **Log Geodesic Biology Reports** on `ReplicatorManager`. **Start Paused**, then resume. Check for pink organisms, **Methanotroph 5** in the HUD and a placement line with `placed=5/5`. Follow `[GeodesicReactionAccounting] metabolism=Methanotrophy`. Compare the measured CH4/O2 inputs, CO2 product, store delta and energy with the equations above. Check that gross totals satisfy the displayed carbon residual tolerance and `zeroMeasuredInputWithEnergy` stays zero. The organism's energy and store should increase as it feeds. Use the lit and unlit sides of the rotating world, or inspect an occupied cell with nonpositive surface insolation in the Unity debugger, to check for positive committed methanotrophy in darkness.
5. For division, rerun with **Carbon-limited division** enabled, **Initial biomass target** `0.0001`, **Division biomass multiple** `1.2`, **Division carbon fraction to child** `0.5`, **Division energy cost** `0`, and **Reproduction rate** `1`. Look for births and an increase above the five founders. For the founding gate, repeat with either initial CH4 or O2 at `0`, keeping vent production and exchange off. Expect no placements. For a feeding-only limitation check, deplete one resource in the occupied node after founding and compare committed transfer and energy before and after, with maintenance still at zero.

### Scenario B — Combined biological methane cycle

1. Use seed `12345`, subdivision `3`, **Ocean World** with minimum depth `0.02`, and a transport interval of `5` simulated seconds. Use the existing multilayer ocean, with positive horizontal and vertical mixing. For a shorter transport path, **Geodesic Ocean Layer Domain → Maximum Layer Count** can be set to `2`. Set the resource field's **Horizontal Mixing Rate** to `0.02` and **Default Vertical Mixing Rate** to `0.05` for this scenario. Keep the CH4 and O2 mixing multipliers positive.
2. In **Ocean**, set initial dissolved **CO2 0.1**, **CH4 0**, **O2 0**, and **Fe2+ 0**. Set the base temperature to `293.15 K` and insolation gain to `15 K`. Use **Terrestrial vent fraction 0**, **Global vent production multiplier 1**, and vent H2, H2S, CO2 and Fe2+ rates of `100 / 0 / 0 / 0` concentration·km³/s. In **Atmosphere**, set initial pressure to `1 bar`, CO2 fraction to `0.01`, O2, CH4, H2 and H2S fractions to `0`, and air–sea gas exchange half-life to `300` simulated seconds. Temporarily set **H2 Oxidation Half Life Seconds** on `GeodesicAbioticChemistry` to `0` to isolate biological O2 uptake from the vent H2 sink.
3. Enable **Oxygenic photosynthesis**, **Methanogenesis**, **Methanotrophy**, and **Passive drift**. Set **Initial replicator count** and **Experimental saprotroph founders** to `0`, **Initial photosynthetic founders** to `40`, **Experimental methanogen founders** to `10`, **Experimental methanotroph founders** to `5`, and **Maximum population** to `100`. Keep **Reproduction rate** and **Basal maintenance energy / sim s** at `0` to isolate the founding and feeding stages. Set **Maximum organic carbon store** to `10`, and the three temperature templates to `0–200 °C`. Use photosynthesis CO2 uptake `0.02` and energy per CO2 `2`, methanogenesis CO2/H2/energy/assimilation `0.01 / 0.02 / 0.03 / 0.15`, and the methanotrophy recipe from Scenario A. Temporarily set `mutationGateMinH2` and `mutationGateMinCO2` on `ReplicatorManager` to `0` and the two methanotrophy acquisition thresholds in **Biology** to `0`. A zero threshold still requires a positive local concentration.
4. Enable the same diagnostics and resume from **Start Paused**. First confirm a photosynthetic O2 product and a methanogenic CH4 product in their respective reaction reports. Then look for local overlap, methanotroph placement and positive measured methanotrophy inputs. Use the Geodesic Cell Picker for layer concentrations and the atmosphere exchange readout. If CH4 reaches layer 0, a negative applied CH4 transfer indicates outgassing. Inspect both dissolved gases in the consumer's current layer to establish local overlap.
5. If **Methanotroph** stays at zero with no placement line, check founder capacity, both positive gases in a single node and the experienced temperature. Also confirm that methanogens found a submarine outlet with exactly zero O2 and that the photosynthetic cohort found a suitable lit site. Placement followed by zero committed extent or energy is a feeding problem. Compare local reactants, temperature, competition and transfer precision. Placement with positive measured feeding followed by population loss is a persistence result. Use the existing birth/death, energy and carbon-store diagnostics to identify the limiting stage. The two producers can remain separated by transport and habitat conditions in this scenario.

## Remaining validation

Unity acceptance is user-run. The milestone uses existing movement and lifecycle behavior. Methanotrophy-specific habitat seeking and long-term population balance are deferred. An optional founder request can remain pending in a world that lacks local overlap or population capacity.
