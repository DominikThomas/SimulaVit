# B2.4 Geodesic methanogenesis

## Reaction and accounting

Hydrogenotrophic methanogens consume dissolved CO2 and H2 in their occupied ocean cell and layer. The catabolic reaction is **CO2 + 4 H2 → CH4 + 2 H2O**. The model tracks methane in the dissolved CH4 pool and omits water. As in the simplified Legacy assimilation model, some CO2 goes to the organism's organic carbon store. The Geodesic model assigns no separate H2 cost to that assimilation.

At full thermal and O2 performance, the configured CO2 uptake and assimilation fraction define the planned catabolic and assimilable shares for a 0.5 simulated-second reference tick. The configured H2 budget caps both shares together at four H2 units per catabolic carbon unit. The recipe requires positive finite CO2, H2 and energy settings and an assimilation fraction below 1. Existing settings retain their saved values. With CO2 set to 0.01, H2 to 0.02, assimilation to 0.15 and energy to 0.03, the H2-limited reference tick can produce approximately 0.005 CH4, store 0.000882 organic carbon, and release 0.015 energy before environmental, competition and precision limits.

Existing node-level competition allocates local CO2 and H2 among consumers. The resource field rechecks both inventories before commit and rounds the store credit down to a representable float. A full store can still support the configured catabolic share. Unused assimilation carbon stays dissolved. The ocean applies CO2, H2 and CH4 together only after planning all three writes and checking the transfer tolerance of `2e-6`. Organism store and energy credits follow the successful commit.

```text
CO2 withdrawn ≈ CH4 produced + increase in stored organic carbon
H2 withdrawn ≈ 4 × CH4 produced
energy gained ≤ min(CH4 produced, H2 withdrawn / 4) × energy parameter / configured CO2 uptake
```

The bounded writes limit rounding residue to the transfer tolerance. Measured CO2 and H2 debits bound the methane credit, and energy rounds down to the representable organism increment. A transfer that fails the precision or inventory checks leaves the reaction uncommitted. CH4 enters the current node's authoritative double-precision inventory. Existing vertical and horizontal transport can move it to other ocean nodes, and the air–sea exchange can transfer it between layer 0 and the finite atmosphere.

## Habitat and evolution

Experienced temperature scales the reaction using the organism's own template and inherited traits. The existing anaerobe controls on `ReplicatorManager` govern reaction efficiency. With `anaerobeO2InhibitionEnabled` and `anaerobeO2InhibitionAffectsMethanogenesis` enabled, the Geodesic anaerobe curve uses the shared comfort and stress concentrations and the methanogenesis-specific minimum efficiency, which defaults to 0. The reaction can run in darkness and uses resources supplied and moved by existing ocean processes, including submarine vents.

Fermentation → Methanogenesis is the only restored edge. Legacy uses the general metabolism mutation roll followed by the fermentation-specific roll, with no timed methanogenesis unlock. Geodesic checks the availability mask and uses a separate world-seeded stream for the two rolls. The daughter needs positive local H2 and CO2 at or above `mutationGateMinH2` and `mutationGateMinCO2`, O2 at or below `mutationGateMaxO2ForAnaerobes`, positive O2 efficiency, and positive experienced-temperature performance in the destination template. These gas thresholds use local dissolved concentrations. The daughter starts from that template and the configured lethal margin before ordinary thermal mutation. Normal trait inheritance and division costs still apply. The independent saprotrophy roll follows and can supersede a successful methane mutation for the same daughter, following Legacy ordering.

## Startup and founders

In **New Game → Biology**, enable **Methanogenesis** under **Biology availability**, or use **All implemented pathways**. **Metabolic evolution** exposes **Fermentation → methanogenesis probability**. **Initial / inherited temperature preferences** exposes the Methanogenesis template. Select **Methanogenesis** in the reaction parameter selector for the H2 budget, CO2 uptake, energy and assimilation. The shared **Maximum organic carbon store** also applies. Legacy Purple remains the base colour and the existing **Methanogen** HUD row counts the restored organisms.

**Experimental methanogen founders** defaults to zero, including when an earlier startup file has no field for it. A positive count requests one batch and requires Methanogenesis and Passive drift. At most once per observed ocean transport tick, a pending request with available population capacity checks compact submarine outlets in their existing order. It uses the first eligible outlet-layer center with exactly zero dissolved O2, sufficient H2 and CO2, and positive temperature performance. It starts the cohort at age zero with ordinary founder energy and an empty carbon store. The batch has a separate deterministic RNG and can be smaller than requested if the cap intervenes. Placement logs `[GeodesicMethanogenFounders]` once. The normal hydrogenotroph founder RNG and existing mutation streams do not consume draws for this feature.

## Limits

Experimental placement is restricted to submarine outlet centers. It can remain pending if no eligible outlet becomes anoxic, sufficiently supplied and thermally viable. Natural emergence depends on a fermenter dividing in an eligible layer, and long-term population persistence depends on the existing resource supply and energy costs. Methanogenesis restores O2 inhibition of uptake. The Legacy oxidative damage timer and O2-specific suppression of reproduction remain Legacy-only. Methanotrophy evolution and movement changes are outside B2.4.

## Manual Unity validation

Back up the startup settings and use temporary scene Inspector values for these checks. Restore them afterwards. Preserve the saved **Hydrogen → fermentation probability** of **0.02** outside the forced evolution check.

### 1. Founder and methane production

1. Start **New Game** with **Geodesic Icosphere**, turn **Use Random Seed** off, and set **Planet Seed** to `12345`. In **Biology**, click **Existing core biology only**, enable **Methanogenesis**, choose **Passive-only movement** and **Founder batch**, set **Initial replicator count** and the other optional founder counts to `0`, **Experimental methanogen founders** to `5`, **Maximum population** to `20`, and **Reproduction rate** to `0`. Set the Methanogenesis temperature template to **0–200 °C** for this check.
2. In **Ocean**, set **Initial Ocean CO2** to `0.1`, initial O2, CH4 and Fe2+ to `0`, **Terrestrial vent fraction** to `0`, and **Global vent production multiplier** to `1`. Use **H2 production (concentration·km³/s)** of `100` and leave CO2 vent production at its configured value. In **Atmosphere**, set atmospheric O2 and CH4 fractions and the **Air–sea gas exchange half-life** to `0`. On `ReplicatorManager`, temporarily set `mutationGateMinH2` and `mutationGateMinCO2` to `0` so any positive concentration can qualify. Enable **Log Geodesic Biology Reports** and **Biology Diagnostics → Enabled**.
3. Click **Start Paused**. Expect **Methanogen 0** before the first supplies arrive. Resume. Look for a single `[GeodesicMethanogenFounders] placed=5/5` message and the purple organisms. Inspect the logged cell and layer for zero O2, positive H2 and CO2, and then dissolved CH4. If the request stays pending, inspect the vent outlet count, injected H2 and the outlet's experienced temperature. Repeat with a maximum population of `2`, then with Methanogenesis and Passive drift disabled individually. Expect two placements at the cap and zero placements with either required trait disabled.

### 2. Ledger, oxygen, light and competition

1. Keep the first setup and set the methanogenesis CO2, H2, energy and assimilation settings to `0.01`, `0.02`, `0.03` and `0.15`. Follow `[GeodesicReactionAccounting] metabolism=Methanogenesis`. Compare `actualAttributedInputs`, `actualAttributedProducts`, `actualStoreDelta` and `energyAwarded/actualDelta` with the ledger above. Check that `measuredAttributedCResidual` is within transfer precision and `zeroMeasuredInputWithEnergy` is zero.
2. For a per-reaction check, attach the Unity debugger and break in `BuildMethanogenesisDemand` and `TryCommitMethanogenesis`. Compare a normal reaction with a full store. At capacity, the methane and energy can still commit while stored carbon stays constant. With several agents in the same node, reduce available H2 in that node under the debugger before demand resolution, then repeat with CO2 scarce. Compare `Desired`, `CompetedActual`, and final `Actual`. Zeroing either input should eliminate methane, carbon and energy credits. With a very small H2 budget relative to the existing inventories, a debit below representable precision should reject the whole transaction.
3. After the founders have appeared, compare the same agent at zero O2 and at the configured O2 stress concentration. Change local O2 in the debugger before the next habitat sample so the founder gate does not intervene. With both inhibition switches on and the methanogen minimum efficiency at zero, the stressed request should have zero extent. Restore O2 to zero. Compare lit and dark conditions while keeping the two substrates and temperature suitable. Both can produce methane.

### 3. Evolution and inheritance

1. Repeat the fixed-seed anoxic setup with **Experimental methanogen founders** at `0`, **Initial replicator count** at `5`, and Hydrogenotrophy, Fermentation and Methanogenesis enabled. Disable Sulfur chemosynthesis, Photosynthesis and Saprotrophy to isolate the two edges. Set **Initial emergence delay (sim s)** to `10` so the normal founder batch has vent H2 to sample. Keep **Carbon-limited division** on. Set **Initial biomass target** to `0.0001`, **Division biomass multiple** to `1.2`, **Division carbon fraction to child** to `0.7`, **Division energy cost (carbon-limited division)** and **Basal maintenance energy / sim s** to `0`, **Reproduction rate** to `1`, and **Maximum population** to `100`. Set the Hydrogenotrophy and Fermentation templates to **0–200 °C** and set **Temperature adaptation mutation chance** to `0`.
2. For this run only, set **General metabolism mutation chance**, **Hydrogen → fermentation probability** and **Fermentation → methanogenesis probability** to `1`. Set `mutationGateMinOrganicC` to `0` on `ReplicatorManager` for the first edge. Break in `TryMutateMethanogenicChild` on a Fermentation parent after it qualifies for division. Check the daughter's cell/layer and resources. Verify a purple methanogen daughter with the configured template and inherited locomotion and biomass target. The parent should retain Fermentation and its inherited traits. If no fermenter reaches division, inspect its energy and inherited organic carbon against the division threshold and check that the population cap still has room.
3. Repeat from the same seed with each mutation probability at zero in turn, with Methanogenesis unavailable, and with the daughter habitat missing H2 or CO2, above the anaerobe O2 gate, or outside the Methanogenesis template plus its lethal margin. Confirm that these cases block methane acquisition. Restore Hydrogen → Fermentation to `0.02` after the forced run.

### 4. Transport, exchange and compatibility

1. Repeat the founder run with positive vertical mixing and **Air–sea gas exchange half-life** at `5` simulated seconds. Keep initial ocean and atmospheric methane zero. Follow dissolved CH4 from the producer layer to L0, then check a rising atmospheric CH4 partial pressure in the HUD. If atmospheric CH4 is too small for the HUD observation, inspect `GeodesicAirSeaGasExchange.GetLastExchangeDiagnostics(GeodesicAtmosphericGas.CH4)` after CH4 reaches L0. A negative `appliedTransferToOceanInventory` is an ocean-to-atmosphere transfer. Ocean totals also reflect transport and exchange, so use the reaction report for the biological ledger.
2. Save and reopen setup with a nonzero methanogen founder count. Load an older settings copy and check that a missing count becomes zero and explicitly saved availability and Hydrogen → Fermentation `0.02` persist. With Methanogenesis disabled and the founder count zero, compare the same seed and settings against B2.3 for the existing founder placement and mutation paths. Start Legacy Cube Sphere with the same saved biology settings and verify its existing evolution and methane parameters are still available.
