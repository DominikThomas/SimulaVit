# B2.2 Geodesic oxygenic photosynthesis

## Startup settings

In **New Game → Biology → Biology availability**, select **Photosynthesis-focused** to enable Hydrogenotrophy and Oxygenic photosynthesis. The preset keeps the selected locomotion types. Enable **Passive drift** for founding and enter a small positive number such as `20` under **Initial photosynthetic founders**. Use **Start Paused** to see the initial green organisms before they drift. The count defaults to `0`, including for older startup JSON files, and is saved separately from the mutation probability and availability masks.

| Founder route | Availability and timing |
|---|---|
| Vent founders | Require Hydrogenotrophy and Passive drift. The existing immediate, delayed or gradual vent rules apply. |
| Photosynthetic founders | Require Oxygenic photosynthesis and Passive drift. A positive count adds a one-time batch at world startup, independent of the vent count, delay, ongoing emergence and evolution unlock. Hydrogenotrophy can be disabled. |

Immediate vent founders are placed first. Photosynthetic founders use the remaining shared population capacity, and their survivors count against capacity for later vent emergence. With Passive drift disabled, both founding routes are blocked even if other locomotion types are available. Later locomotion mutation and trait inheritance use the existing B2.1 controls.

The photosynthetic batch considers the shallowest eligible layer in each ocean cell. Eligible sites have positive local light and dissolved CO2 at or above the existing mutation gate thresholds, finite positive layer volume, and positive thermal performance in the Photosynthesis template. Founding also requires positive, finite photosynthetic uptake, energy yield and carbon storage capacity. A separate world-seeded random stream selects sites without replacement each pass and initializes age-zero founders with the normal founder energy range and zero stored carbon. If some sites must be reused to reach the count, founders can share a node. If no site qualifies or the population cap is reached, fewer founders are placed. The existing `[GeodesicBiologyAvailability]` startup line reports the placed count when requested. The zero-count path leaves vent placement and existing random streams untouched.

## Evolution and presentation

Hydrogenotrophs must first pass ordinary division and the **General metabolism mutation chance**. Once `PlanetGenerator.PhotosynthesisUnlocked` is true, the existing **Hydrogen → photosynthesis probability** can acquire the enabled trait in the daughter's inherited ocean layer. The light and dissolved CO2 gates use `mutationGateMinLight` and `mutationGateMinCO2` from the manager. The birth position must also be within the Photosynthesis template's thermal tolerance. The daughter stays in the inherited cell and layer. A successful photosynthesis acquisition takes priority. Otherwise the existing sulfur and fermentation ordering applies. There is no reverse transition in Geodesic mode.

The mutant rebases its temperature traits to the configured **Photosynthesis** range and default lethal margin before the existing individual thermal mutations. Same-metabolism descendants inherit and adapt their parent's individual traits as before. The existing green color, growth rendering, division animation and conservative division transfers apply to photosynthetic organisms.

## Light and reaction accounting

The runtime reuses the Photosynthesis enum, its `BuildRequest` case, the light cache, and the saved CO2-at-full-light and energy-per-CO2 settings. The surface temperature field supplies the current cosine between the cell normal and the synchronized planet-to-sun direction. The existing `ResolvePhotosyntheticLight` clamps the cosine to daylight and multiplies it by `1.0` at layer 0, `0.55` at layer 1, and `0` deeper down. The same calculation gates founding and mutation. An unavailable or invalid solar direction yields darkness. The optional startup batch refreshes that direction from the visible sun after the orbit reset, before choosing sites.

During the existing reaction tick, full-light CO2 demand scales with local light, organism thermal performance and tick duration relative to the 0.5 simulated-second reference tick. Free organism carbon storage caps demand. Requests share available CO2 through the existing competition step. The resource field then measures the proposed CO2 debit and O2 credit in inventory units (concentration times layer volume). A successful transaction stores the corresponding carbon and produces equal O2 within the existing reaction precision tolerance. The resource field commits both writes together and credits energy from the matched transfer at the configured yield, rounded down to representable organism energy. Invalid, unrepresentable or blocked transfers leave ocean inventories and organism stores unchanged. Existing reaction accounting reports measured CO2, O2, carbon and energy. Division and death recycling retain their existing carbon accounting.

## Limits of this milestone

Darkness stops photosynthetic production while normal maintenance continues. Existing stores and energy can still support division under the standard gates. Night respiration, dark anoxic survival, light-seeking movement and ecological balancing remain deferred. Founders may subsequently drift into darkness or deeper water. Day length, available CO2, temperature, maintenance and carbon storage all affect persistence. Ocean mixing, air–sea exchange and O2 sinks can mask the biological signal in whole-ocean totals.

## Manual Unity validation

Back up the saved startup settings before changing them for these checks.

1. **Founders and availability.** Use a fixed Geodesic seed and start paused with `20` photosynthetic founders. Set the vent count to `0`, disable ongoing vent emergence and Hydrogenotrophy, and leave Oxygenic photosynthesis and Passive drift enabled. Confirm that the reported count and green organisms agree. Inspect their cells and layers for positive light, available CO2 and suitable temperature. Repeat with the same seed and settings to compare placements. Toggle Oxygenic photosynthesis and Passive drift off individually and confirm each blocks the batch.
2. **Save and zero-count regression.** Save and reopen setup to check that the count and the existing mutation settings persist, including Hydrogen → Fermentation at `0.02`. Load a pre-B2.2 configuration and confirm the count is `0`. With the count at zero and Photosynthesis unavailable, compare a fixed-seed vent startup with B2.1 using the same founder mode and settings, including founder positions, traits and initial population.
3. **Evolution in isolation.** Set photosynthetic founders to `0`, enable Hydrogenotrophy and Oxygenic photosynthesis, and temporarily set the general and Hydrogen → Photosynthesis probabilities to `1`. After the unlock time, observe hydrogenotroph divisions in eligible shallow, illuminated water with CO2. The parents should stay hydrogenotrophic while eligible daughters can turn green and use the Photosynthesis template. With thermal mutation temporarily set to zero, compare exact template endpoints. Repeat with the destination disabled and with births in dark, deep or CO2-poor layers to check the gates. Vent-centered parents may need to reach the photic layer before this can occur.
4. **Day, night and depth.** Follow a green organism and inspect the local light factor with the cell picker. Confirm that it drops to zero at night and below layer 1, and that layer 1 receives 55% of the light at layer 0 for the same cell. Check that photosynthetic reaction credit stops in darkness while maintenance continues. Watch successful daytime growth and division with the existing visuals.
5. **Resource accounting.** Enable **Biology Diagnostics → Enabled** and **Log Geodesic Biology Reports** on `ReplicatorManager`. For an illuminated photosynthesis-only cohort, inspect `[GeodesicReactionAccounting]` for `metabolism=Photosynthesis`. Check that attributed CO2 withdrawal, stored carbon and attributed O2 output agree within tolerance and that energy awarded matches the reaction energy increment. Compare darkness, depleted CO2 and exhausted carbon storage. Check that these conditions grant no photosynthetic credit and leave inventories nonnegative. Use the reaction accounting to distinguish these transfers from chemistry and exchange.
